Methodology and limitations

Measure → interpret → verify

01 / Sample

Random byte-block sampling of large JSONL files; join review and metadata records by parent ASIN.

02 / Analyze

Lexical aspect taxonomy, clause-level VADER sentiment, low-star lift, TF-IDF listing comparison, exploratory LDA.

03 / Augment

Selective GPT-4o mini review interpretation with constrained schemas, caching and cost controls.

04 / Validate

Review ID and verbatim evidence checks, lexical fallback, and a separate human-label evaluation template.

What the sample can show

How an evidence-grounded decision-support pipeline behaves on a reproducible historical sample; examples of complaint signals and testable product/listing hypotheses.

What it cannot establish

Population prevalence, causal product defects, live listing alignment, validated model accuracy, financial impact, or operational production readiness.
